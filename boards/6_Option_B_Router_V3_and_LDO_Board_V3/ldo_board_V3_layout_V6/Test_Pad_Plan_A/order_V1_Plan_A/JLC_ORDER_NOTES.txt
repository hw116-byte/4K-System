=== PCB REQUIREMENTS (full list) ===
6-layer 1-step HDI, stack-up JLCH061611Y1-1078, 1 oz all layers, ENIG. Please confirm the stack-up or name the nearest one.
Vias: laser 0.10 mm hole / 0.26 mm pad on L1-L2 (218) and L5-L6 (124), copper filled and planarised;
mechanical buried on L2-L5: 72 x 0.20 / 0.40 mm and 116 x 0.15 / 0.35 mm (the same drill and pad as the through vias), resin plugged and capped; through 0.15 / 0.35 mm L1-L6 (58), epoxy filled and capped.
Stacked vias: 151 laser vias sit on the axis of a buried via (86 on L1-L2, 65 on L5-L6); at 51 of these places the stack is three high (L1-L2 laser, L2-L5 buried, L5-L6 laser on one axis).
  The buried vias must be plugged and copper capped flat so that the laser vias land on the cap.
Vias in pads: 202 laser vias and 38 through-via ends sit in SMD pads (0.4 mm pitch connector pads, DFN / SOT-23-5 and chip pads, test pads). These pads must come out flat and solderable.
Drill files: -PTH.drl = through L1-L6 (58 x 0.15 mm vias); -front-in1.drl = laser L1-L2 (218); -in4-back.drl = laser L5-L6 (124); -in1-in4.drl = buried L2-L5 (72 x 0.20 mm, 116 x 0.15 mm). There are no non-plated holes and no slots.
Layer order: .gtl = L1, .g1 = L2, .g2 = L3, .g3 = L4, .g4 = L5, .gbl = L6.
Minimum track / space 0.10 / 0.10 mm, hole to copper 0.20 mm, copper to board edge 0.30 mm.
Silkscreen: text 1.0 mm high with 0.15 mm stroke (titles 1.2 / 0.18 mm). Part outlines from the KiCad library are drawn with 0.12 mm lines; please widen them to your minimum.
No part overhangs the board outline; the closest part courtyard is 0.17 mm from the edge (C16).
Please add no balancing copper inside the board outline.
Please send the production file for confirmation before fabrication.

=== ASSEMBLY REQUIREMENTS (full list) ===
Double-sided Standard PCBA, 92 placements, 22 BOM lines: 83 bottom, 9 top (C701, C702, J1, J2, J5, J6, R4, R23, U7).
