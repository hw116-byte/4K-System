B6Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely cause and what to do
Red LED next to a portThe port draws over 0.64 A, is too hot, or is fed back from the head; the switch keeps retrying. Unplug the head, then check its cable and boards.
No port powers upThe 5 V supply is not within 94–107 % of its target. Check the adapter, and measure the 5 V on C704 pin 1.
TP14 below 3.6 V under loadThe cable's resistance is too high. Use the 3 ft cable or one with thicker wires.
FPGA does not load (TP12 DONE stays low)A supply is missing (TP10 BOOT_RAILS_PG_3V3 low), or the flash memory has no program. Check TP2–TP9, then load the program over JTAG.
No recorded data reaches the PCThe data polarity or the data rate is wrong. Set TXPOLARITY = 1 in the Frontend program, and use the same data rate at both ends.
Cannot program a head (JTAG)The cable lacks pin 2 (use a cable “with Ethernet”), or VIO1 is not 1.8 V. Check the cable's continuity and set VIO1.
