ASIC boards V15 (top board and bottom board) - notes for the PCBWay order
Written 19 Nov 2026. Nothing has been uploaded or ordered. Two different boards: order them as two items.

FILES
  ASIC_Top_Board_V15_Gerbers.zip      ASIC_Top_Board_V15_BOM.csv      ASIC_Top_Board_V15_CPL.csv
  ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15_Gerbers.zip   ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15_BOM.csv   ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15_CPL.csv
  Each Gerber ZIP has 21 files: 10 copper layers (F_Cu, In1_Cu ... In8_Cu, B_Cu), top and bottom solder mask, paste and
  silkscreen, Edge_Cuts, PTH.drl, NPTH.drl (empty), front-in1.drl (laser vias, layer 1 to layer 2) and the Gerber job file.

BOARD (as drawn in the KiCad files)
  Size 22.37 x 17.03 mm, 10 copper layers, finished thickness 0.8 mm, surface finish ENEPIG (the dies are wire bonded).
  Stack in the file, top to bottom (mm): F.Cu 0.035 / prepreg 0.06 / In1 0.0175 / core 0.05 / In2 0.0175 / prepreg 0.10 /
  In3 0.0175 / core 0.05 / In4 0.0175 / prepreg 0.05 / In5 0.0175 / core 0.05 / In6 0.0175 / prepreg 0.10 / In7 0.0175 /
  core 0.05 / In8 0.0175 / prepreg 0.06 / B.Cu 0.035, plus 0.01 solder mask each side. Please propose your own stack if this
  one cannot be built in 0.8 mm; no impedance-controlled lines are specified on these boards.
  Through vias: 402 per board, 0.15 mm hole, 0.305 mm pad (one is 0.3026 mm).
  Laser vias, F.Cu to In1 only: 126 on the top board, 125 on the bottom board, 0.10 mm hole, 0.255 mm pad.
  Every via is to be filled and capped (plated over); many sit in or beside pads. Vias are covered by solder mask.
  Smallest track and gap 0.0762 mm (3 mil). Hole to copper at least 0.1524 mm. Copper to board edge at least 0.20 mm.
  Solder mask colour and silkscreen colour are not set in the files.

QUESTIONS FOR PCBWAY BEFORE THE ORDER (not yet answered)
  1. Can you build 10 layers in 0.8 mm with this via structure (through + one laser span, all filled and capped)?
  2. Your help page asks for 0.30 mm between vias of different nets. Measured edge to edge, 117 pairs (top board) and
     111 pairs (bottom board) are closer than 0.30 mm, the closest 0.233 mm; centre to centre every pair is at least 0.359 mm.
     Which measurement applies, and is this layout accepted?
  3. Are 3 mil tracks and gaps and ENEPIG accepted on this build?

ASSEMBLY (33 parts per board: 31 capacitors, J3, J4)
  NOT fitted by the assembler: U01_BCD1 and U01_BCD2 (the two bare dies; they are attached and wire bonded separately)
  and J1. They are not in the BOM or the placement file. No paste is printed on the die bond pads (352 pads per board,
  mask open, no paste). Keep the bond pads clean: no flux residue, no marking ink.
  J3, bottom side: Hirose DF40C-80DS-0.4V(51), receptacle, HRS No. CL0684-4002-0-51.
  J4, top side:    Hirose DF40C-80DP-0.4V(51), plug,       HRS No. CL0684-4001-8-51.
  Mated (stacking) height of this pair: 1.5 mm. Source: Hirose DF40 Series Catalog, Jan. 2025, pages 6, 8 and 14,
  https://www.hirose.com/en/product/series/DF40/documents?path=%2Fapi%2Fv1%2Fseries%2FDF40%2Fdocuments%2Fproduct_series_catalog%2Fcontent%3Flang%3DEN
  No substitutes for J3 and J4 without asking: other DF40 receptacles (DF40HC and the 2.0 to 4.0 mm types) change the stack height.
  Pin 1 of each connector is marked on the board by a small triangle and a 0.65 mm dot beside it.
  Capacitors: 15 x 10 nF Murata GRM033R71E103KE14D and 16 x 100 nF Samsung CL03A104KA3NNNC, both 0201.

TELLING THE TWO BOARDS APART
  Each board is marked on both faces: "TOP" or "BOTTOM", and "V15". One recording head uses two TOP and two BOTTOM boards.
  Please keep the two types in separate, labelled bags.
