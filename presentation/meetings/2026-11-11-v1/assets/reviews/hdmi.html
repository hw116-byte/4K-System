HDMI-46Minor

U700's output capacitors C704-C707 are X5R parts rated to 85 C, not the X7R/X6S/X7S types TI lists.

C704-C707 are Murata GRM32ER61C476KE15L (47 uF 16 V X5R 1210, JLC C77101), rated -55 to +85 C. TI's recommended output capacitors for the TPSM63606 are X7R, X6S, X7S or X6T types. C704 and C705 sit next to U700. Capacitance is still enough: about 30 uF each at 5 V against TI's 30 uF total minimum.

Where
C704 (121.8, 65.0), C705 (126.7, 65.0), C706 (121.8, 68.4), C707 (126.7, 68.4), top side, net FE_5V.
If left alone
If the area beside U700 runs above 85 C, the capacitors operate outside their rating and age faster. Failure would be gradual.
What to do
BOM-only change: fit an X7R 47 uF 1210 such as Murata GRM32ER71A476KE15L (C84494) on the same pads. Log the temperature beside U700 at bring-up.
Source

HDMI-47Minor

A single fault in U700's feedback can put up to about 12 V on FE_5V, and nothing on FE_5V limits it.

FE_5V is set only by R700 (40.2 kOhm) and R701 (10 kOhm) on U700's FB pin, and U700's power-good, which enables all eight ports, also judges FB. FE_5V has 35 pads and no clamp, fuse or overvoltage cut-off. An open R700, a grounded FB or a failed module drives FE_5V towards the 12 V input; power-good then turns the ports off, but the eight TPS2553 switches (7 V maximum) are left holding that voltage. A wrong divider value (for example R700 about 55-115 kOhm) is worse: power-good stays high and every head gets 6.5-11.9 V, above the 6.5 V maximum of its U16 regulator.

Where
U700 pin 10 FB (Net-(U700-FB) = U700.10, R700.1, R701.2, C708.2); PORT_ENABLE = U700.13, R703, U701-U708 pin 3; FE_5V.
If left alone
One wrong 0603 resistor, one solder bridge or one module failure can overstress all eight port switches, or damage up to eight head stacks at once.
What to do
First power-up rule: power J4 with no cables, check FE_5V reads 4.9-5.15 V (at C704 pin 1, see HDMI-59) and TP701-TP708 read the same, and stop if not; repeat after any rework of U700, R700 or R701. In a later revision, add an overvoltage cut-off on FE_5V rated for the 12-15 V input.
Source
Netlist from HDMI_Extension_Board_V39.kicad_sch; TI TPSM63606 SLVSGB4B sections 7.5 and 8.3.9, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpsm63606.pdf ; TI TPS2553 SLVS841G section 6.1, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf

HDMI-01Note

Each port's current limit leaves little margin over the high-case head load, and pin 19 carries it alone.

Each port switch (TPS2553, R711-R718 = 37.4 kOhm) limits at 630-771 mA. The recorded high-case head load is 0.55 A at the 5 V pin, and the head's buck regulator draws more current as cable resistance lowers its input voltage. The stimulation supply on the FPGA-LDO board ramps for about 8 ms at start-up and adds roughly 0.1-0.3 A at 5 V while it does. All of this flows through the single pin-19 contact, which Molex rates 0.8 A at 25 C and 0.3 A per contact with all 19 contacts powered at 55 C.

Where
U701-U708 pin 5 with R711-R718; PORT1_5V-PORT8_5V to J610-J617 pin 19; Frontend J3.19 VIN_5V to U16.
If left alone
With a high-load head, the port may sit in current limit, mainly during start-up, so the head browns out or restarts. Pin 19 at both cable ends may run warmer than Molex has qualified.
What to do
On the first stack, log the port current from cable insertion to 100 ms and in steady recording and stimulation, measure the cable's pin-19 loop resistance, and check pin-19 temperature after 30 min at the highest load.
Source
TI TPS2553 SLVS841G eq. 1-3, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf ; Molex PS-46765-003 secs. 4.2 and 5.1 (distributor copy https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/026/DOC027026286.pdf); ADI LT3582 data sheet p.14, https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/3582512fb.pdf

HDMI-24Note

All eight ports switch together on U700's power-good; the FPGA cannot switch, reset or monitor a port.

PORT_ENABLE connects only U700 pin 13 (PG), R703 and the eight switch EN pins. The switch FAULT outputs drive only the bottom-side LEDs. No XEM pin or header reads them or drives the enables.

If left alone
A hung head can only be power-cycled by pulling its cable or the 12 V input, and one dip on FE_5V restarts all eight heads at once.
What to do
Nothing for this build. In a later revision, route the port enables and a combined FAULT signal to spare XEM pins.

HDMI-58Note

A shorted or very low R711-R718 silently removes that port's current limit.

Each port's limit is set only by its ILIM resistor (37.4 kOhm). TI specifies the limit only for 15-232 kOhm; at 15 kOhm it is already 1.6-1.8 A. A bridged or wrong low-value resistor shows no symptom in normal use.

Where
U701-U708 pin 5 (ILIM) to R711-R718 pin 2; R71x pin 1 to DGND (bottom side).
If left alone
A later short in that port's cable or head then pushes several amps through pin 19 and the thin cable wire, or pulls U700 into its limit so all eight heads restart repeatedly.
What to do
Before first power, measure 36-39 kOhm from each U701-U708 pin 5 to ground.

Netlist from HDMI_Extension_Board_V39.kicad_sch; TI TPS2553 SLVS841G sections 6.3 and 8.5.1, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf

Signals, clocks & timing 3

HDMI-05Minor

U602 drives the FT232H TDO pin while the FT232H, in its default UART mode, also drives that pin.

Level shifter U602 (TXU0304) drives HOST_TDO from a push-pull output (pin 5) straight into FT232H U501 pin 15 (ADBUS2), with no series resistor; only R509 (10 kOhm pull-up) is also on the net. U602 is enabled through DRIVE_ENABLE whenever SW601 is at ENABLE and the selected target's reference voltage is valid. In asynchronous UART mode, which the FT232H uses with a blank EEPROM such as the fitted U505, ADBUS2 is the RTS# output. The two outputs then fight until software switches the FT232H to its JTAG (MPSSE) mode.

Where
Net HOST_TDO = U501.15, U602.5, R509.1; U602.8 OE = DRIVE_ENABLE (U605.1); U505 93LC56B EEPROM.
If left alone
Before the EEPROM is programmed, or whenever the FT232H sits in UART mode with a target enabled, both drivers short into each other. This stresses U602 and U501 beyond their ratings but is not an instant failure.
What to do
Keep SW601 at OFF until the U505 EEPROM is programmed for JTAG use. At the next layout change, add a 330 Ohm to 1 kOhm series resistor between U602 pin 5 and U501 pin 15.
Source
Netlist from HDMI_Extension_Board_V39.kicad_sch; TI TXU0304 SCES935A https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/txu0304.pdf ; FTDI UM232H-B data sheet pin table (ADBUS2 = RTS# in UART mode) https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/163/DS_UM232H-B-218263.pdf ; Linux ftdi_sio note on the UART default https://lore.kernel.org/all/20170719132327.2ddb8fbe@crub/T/
Confirmed in the files
HDMI-06Note

Frontend JTAG through the cable is estimated to work only up to about 6 MHz TCK, below Vivado's common 15 MHz default.

The TDO answer must return within half a TCK period. The loop adds U602 both ways (19 + 14 ns), the cable both ways, the FPGA clock-to-TDO time (7 ns), a heavily loaded 225 mm TDO trunk and the FT232H setup time (11 ns). With the 0.9 m cable this is about 80 ns, so TCK is limited to about 6 MHz.

Where
JTAG_TCK_1V8 / JTAG_TDO_1V8 trunks from U602 to U610-U618; R640-R671; J610-J617 pins 17/18.
If left alone
At 15 MHz, scans or programming of a head may fail intermittently, which looks like a cable or board fault.
What to do
Start Frontend JTAG at 3-6 MHz in hw_server and step up while checking IDCODE reads; record the working limit.
Source
TI TXU0304 SCES935A section 7.11, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/txu0304.pdf ; AMD DS181 boundary-scan timing; AMD UG908 JTAG clock frequency; cable delay about 5 ns/m (engineering judgement)

HDMI-44Note

Y500's load capacitors may pull the FT232H clock 20-55 ppm fast, beyond FTDI's +/-30 ppm.

Y500 (SJK 7U12000E18UCG) is specified for an 18 pF load, but two 18 pF capacitors plus pin and trace capacitance give it only about 11-14 pF. The estimated pull is 20-55 ppm fast, before the crystal's own tolerance. FTDI asks for 12 MHz within +/-30 ppm. The same values are on Opal Kelly's BRK8305 and USB is expected to work.

Where
Y500 on U501 OSCI/OSCO; C503, C504 (KEMET C0402C180J5GACTU, 18 pF).
If left alone
The FT232H runs outside its stated clock tolerance and USB has less margin. Enumeration will most likely still work.
What to do
Check USB enumeration on the first boards. If it is unreliable, fit 27 pF C0G 0402 for C503/C504 on the same pads.
Source
SJK 7U12000E18UCG spec (LCSC copy), https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/5264ef3486a068e24cbf9f4599b78983.pdf?productCode=C524717 ; FTDI DS_FT232H section 6.3, https://ftdichip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/DS_FT232H.pdf

Layout & manufacturing 10

HDMI-07Minor

Several bypass capacitors of the USB-JTAG chips U501 (FT232H) and U602 sit 3-10 mm from their supply pins instead of right beside them.

U602 (TXU0304 level shifter): TI asks for a 0.1 uF capacitor on each supply pin, as close to the part as possible. The nearest capacitor to pin 1 (FTDI3V3) is C604 at 10.4 mm; the nearest to pin 14 (+1.8V) are C603 and C605 at 9.6 mm. +1.8V here is 0.2-0.3 mm track with no plane. U501 (FT232H USB-to-JTAG chip, top side): all of its regulator, PLL and PHY capacitors are on the bottom side. VREGIN pin 40 reaches C501 (100 nF) at 7.0 mm and C500 (4.7 uF) at 9.1 mm through about 8.2 mm of 0.2 mm track and a via, on a stub away from the FB502 bead that feeds the pin. VPLL pin 8 reaches C514/C513 at 5.4/6.3 mm on a separate stub, while its FB500 bead feeds the pin through 14.3 mm of 0.15 mm L3 track. VPHY pin 3 reaches C515/C516 at 2.9/4.2 mm. Opal Kelly's BRK8305 breakout, the source of this FT232H circuit, places the same capacitors on the top side 1.4-1.9 mm from the pins. Of the capacitors on U501's regulator, PLL and PHY pins, only C517 (VCORE) and C518 (VCCA) sit close, at 0.5 and 1.8 mm.

Where
U602 (bottom, 146.0, 75.75): pin 1 at (143.14, 77.70), pin 14 at (148.86, 77.70); C603, C604, C605 (bottom). U501 (top, about 162, 57): pin 40 VREGIN at (163.25, 52.98) with C500/C501 (bottom, 154.52/156.72, 50.50); pin 8 VPLL at (157.97, 57.75) with C513/C514 (bottom, 154.52/156.72, 52.50); pin 3 VPHY with C515/C516 (bottom); beads FB500-FB502 (bottom, x 171.7-175.6).
If left alone
At U602, supply bounce when the outputs switch can add noise or false edges on the JTAG lines, including TCK; the risk is low at JTAG speeds. At U501, trace and via inductance ahead of any bypass adds supply noise to the USB High-Speed PHY and PLL, which can show up as unreliable High-Speed enumeration or bit errors on the built-in programmer that loads the XEM8310 and all eight Frontends. Both will most likely still work.
What to do
On the first board, check that the FT232H enumerates at High Speed (480 Mb/s) and run a long JTAG readback at the intended TCK. At the next layout change, add 100 nF capacitors right beside U602 pins 1 and 14, and move C500/C501, C513/C514 and C515/C516 next to U501 pins 40, 8 and 3 (top side as on BRK8305, or directly under the pins), routing each bead to its capacitor and then to the pin.

HDMI_Extension_Board_V39.kicad_pcb (pad positions and tracks); TI TXU0304 SCES935A section 12.1, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/txu0304.pdf ; FTDI DS_FT232H (pin functions), https://ftdichip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/DS_FT232H.pdf ; Opal Kelly BRK8305 rev A03 layout (reference for the FT232H circuit)

HDMI-08Minor

The U801-U816 ESD-array pads do not follow TI's pattern: signal pads are wider and ground pads narrower.

U801-U816 are TPD4E02B04 arrays in a 1.0 x 2.5 mm 10-pad package at 0.5 mm pitch. All ten pads are 0.30 x 0.55 mm, so adjacent signal pads are 0.20 mm apart. TI's example uses 0.20 mm signal pads (0.30 mm gaps) and 0.40 mm ground pads (pins 3 and 8). The ground pads get about half TI's paste.

Where
U801-U816 (footprint USON-10_2.5x1.0mm_P0.5mm), top side near x 75 mm.
If left alone
A solder bridge would short a cable signal line (recording, clock, command or SYNC) to its neighbour or to ground and is hard to see under the part. The added risk over TI's pattern is modest.
What to do
Ask JLC at file review whether the pads and the 0.22 x 0.47 mm paste openings are acceptable, or narrow the signal pads to 0.20-0.25 mm and widen pads 3/8 to 0.40 mm at the next layout change.
Source
HDMI_Extension_Board_V39.kicad_pcb (U801 pads); TI TPD4E02B04 SLVSD85B pp.24-25, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd4e02b04.pdf

HDMI-60Minor

JLC must confirm several order points in writing before production.

(1) In the JLC cart JLC adds its own 5 mm rails on the long edges (board becomes 167.65 x 124.1 mm), but the order note asks for rails on the left and right only: the bottom long edge is the gold-finger edge that needs the bevel, and J500 overhangs the top edge by about 1.2 mm. (2) The order form offers only 'Remove Mark' or a 2D barcode, not the order-number position marked on the bottom silk at (175, -131). (3) JLC's thickness tolerance for a 1.6 mm board is +/-10 %, 1.44-1.76 mm, while the note needs 1.44-1.70 mm at the fingers. (4) L3 has no copper pour (about 9 % copper) against about 93 % on L2, L4 and L5; the note invites JLC to propose balancing copper but sets no bow/twist limit. (5) The note asks for written acceptance of the 210 vias with 0.15 mm drills (about 10.6:1 in the 1.594 mm board). These vias (0.25 mm pad) and the 142 vias with 0.20 mm drills (0.30 mm pad) have a 0.05 mm annular ring, JLC's minimum; JLC prefers the pad 0.15 mm larger than the hole. The note does not mention the rings.

Where
Board outline (167.65 x 114.10 mm); J6 finger edge y 162.05; J500 at the top edge; L3 (In2.Cu); the 352 smallest vias (210 x 0.15/0.25 mm, 142 x 0.20/0.30 mm); order note sections PCB 1, 2, 4, 5, Silkscreen and Assembly 1.
If left alone
Rails on the long edges conflict with the finger bevel and the overhanging USB-C connector. A board thicker than 1.70 mm at the fingers may not seat in the slot. Uneven copper can bow the card. Rings at the minimum leave no margin for drill wander in the deep 0.15 mm holes. Without answers these points are settled by JLC, not by the design.
What to do
At file review, get JLC to confirm in writing: rails on the left and right edges only, the order-number position, 1.44-1.70 mm at the fingers, any L3 balancing copper (kept 1.0 mm from pairs) with a bow/twist limit, the 0.15 mm drills, and the 0.05 mm rings on the 0.15/0.25 mm and 0.20/0.30 mm vias.

Parts, BOM & sourcing 1

HDMI-13Minor

Some part fields name a different part from the one JLC fits, and this board keeps its JLC numbers in a 'JLCPCB' field instead of 'LCSC'.

(1) The C6 BOM line reads 'TMK105BJ104KV-F, C6, C77014', and the schematic gives the same Taiyo Yuden part number. JLC C77014 is Murata GRM155R71E104KE14D, an equivalent 100 nF 25 V 0402 capacitor (X7R instead of X5R). (2) The Datasheet links of U700's feedback and timing resistors point to other makers' parts. R700 (40.2 kOhm) links JLC C308258 (ROHM MCR03EZPFX4022) but is ordered as C137723 (Yageo RC0603FR-0740K2L). R702 (13 kOhm) links C4083262 (Panasonic ERJ-H3EF1302V) but is ordered as C22797 (UNI-ROYAL 0603WAF1302T5E). Values and packages match. (3) All 270 fitted parts carry their JLC C-number (the JLC/LCSC part number) in a field named 'JLCPCB'. The Frontend, FPGA-LDO and ASIC boards use 'LCSC', as the project's parts rule asks. The numbers themselves are complete and match the order BOM.

Where
C6 (+VDCIN bypass), order BOM line 'TMK105BJ104KV-F,C6,BRK_C6,C77014'; R700 (U700 FB, 40.2 kOhm), R702 (U700 RT, 13 kOhm); 'JLCPCB' field on all 270 fitted symbols and footprints.
