Pin map & connectors 1

LDOA-64Minor

The port capacitors and safe-idle pull resistors are drawn away from the connectors they serve, joined only by net labels.

The 6 port capacitors C51 and C53-C57 (1 uF on six ASIC rails) and the 18 pull resistors R2-R6 and R16-R28, which hold the ASIC control lines at a safe level, each reach their net only through a 5 to 10 mm wire stub and a net label. The other pin has a power symbol or a shared ground or label stub. None is wired to a J1-J4 pin or to a local bus there: the capacitors sit at x 345 to 406 and the pulls at x 403 to 715, while J1/J2 are at x 74.93 and J3/J4 at x 270.51. The C51 note box itself says C51, C53, C55 and C57 sit at the J3/J4 ports. The netlist is correct: ERC (KiCad's schematic rule check) reports 0 errors, and the schematic-to-layout check finds 0 differences. The project's drawing rule asks for support parts to be wired visibly to the pins they serve and kept beside that circuit.

Where
Schematic V43: C51, C53-C57 (x 345-406, y 348-371); R2-R5, R16-R19 (x 600-677, y 40-65); R20-R28 (x 403-535, y 472-497); R6 (715, 114); J1/J2 at x 74.93, J3/J4 at x 270.51.
If left alone
No electrical effect. A reader at a connector cannot see which pins are pulled up, pulled down or decoupled, so checking the safe-idle levels against the pin map is slow and error-prone.
What to do
In the next schematic revision, redraw the pull resistors beside the J1-J4 pins they serve and the port capacitors beside J3/J4, with continuous wires to those pins or a short local bus, keeping their note boxes beside them. Keep the netlist identical and rerun ERC and DRC with schematic parity.

LDOA-62Minor

The DF40 board-to-board connectors get full-size solder-paste openings, and the order does not set the top-side stencil thickness.

J1, J2 and J3 (top) and J4 (bottom) are Hirose DF40 connectors at 0.4 mm pitch. No paste reduction is set on the board, the footprints or the pads, so every paste opening is the full pad: 0.23 x 0.66 mm on the top plugs (0.17 mm between pads) and 0.20 x 0.70 mm on J4, as the order Gerbers confirm. The assembly remark asks for a 0.10 mm stencil on the bottom only and says nothing about the top, where J1-J3 are three of the six parts. Hirose recommends a 0.12 mm stencil with 80 % openings for DF40 and allows at most two reflow cycles.

Where
Top: J1 (91.25, 127.0), J2 (108.75, 127.0), J3 (99.78, 113.51); bottom: J4 (99.78, 113.52). Assembly remark, 'Bottom stencil' line.
If left alone
If JLC uses a 0.12 mm top stencil and does not shrink the openings itself, J1-J3 get about 125 % of Hirose's paste volume. That raises the chance of solder bridges between pads, which are hard to see once the connector is soldered. J4 at 0.10 mm with full openings is about equal to Hirose's figure. In this two-sided build the connector on the side soldered first already goes through two reflows, so reflow rework of that connector would exceed Hirose's limit.
What to do
In the assembly remark, ask for a 0.10 mm stencil on both sides, or reduce the DF40 paste openings to about 80 % in the footprints and regenerate the paste Gerbers. Ask JLC not to rework DF40 joints by reflow.

LDOA-31Note

The board's DRC setup leaves the mask-web and silkscreen-clearance limits at 0 and ignores seven check types, so DRC would not flag some later mistakes.

DRC is KiCad's layout rule check. The minimum solder-mask web is set to 0 mm. The smallest mask web on the board lies between 0.16 and 0.18 mm (DRC at 0.16 mm is clean, at 0.18 mm it reports 199 bridges at the DF40 pads), above JLC's 0.10 mm green limit. The minimum silkscreen clearance is also 0, and the project ignores seven check types: footprint library mismatch and library issues, footprint filter and type mismatch, missing courtyard, track not centred on a via, and tuning-profile geometry. With those set to warning and a 0.15 mm silkscreen clearance, DRC reports 89 more items, all harmless today. 64 are footprint mismatches in which the pads match the project library; on the DFN and X2SON regulators the only pad difference is the bottom-side mirroring. 14 are track ends 0.002 to 0.15 mm off a via centre but inside the via pad. 8 are silkscreen items closer than 0.15 mm: seven where the pin-1 dots of U2, U4, U6 and U8 sit 0.128 to 0.135 mm from part outlines, and one where the 'NT1' text is 0.143 mm from the 'A' label. The last 3 are on J2, whose pin-1 silk triangle comes within 0.09 mm of the board edge.

Where
Board setup: solder mask minimum web, minimum silkscreen clearance, rule severities. Top: J2 pin-1 silk triangle at x 110.71 to 111.23, y 119.91 to 120.49, against the east edge at x 111.32. Bottom: pin-1 dots of U2, U4, U6, U8.
If left alone
Edge routing may clip part of J2's pin-1 triangle, which marks the connector's orientation. Otherwise no effect on the current files, but a mask sliver, footprint drift or off-centre track added by a later edit would pass DRC.
What to do
Set the minimum mask web to 0.10 mm (DRC stays clean at that value) and the minimum silkscreen clearance to 0.15 mm in the board setup, move J2's pin-1 triangle at least 0.15 mm in from the edge, and turn the footprint-mismatch and via-centring checks back on as warnings.

LDOA-65Note

U7's thermal pad has a laser via at its centre, which TI's data sheet advises against for this tiny package.

A 0.10 mm L5-L6 laser via (0.26 mm pad, AGND) sits at the centre of pad 5, the 0.58 mm thermal pad of U7, inside its paste opening. U7 is the 1 x 1 mm TPS7A2015 that makes VDD_CGEN. U3, U4 and U5 have no via in this pad. TI says not to put a via directly under the thermal pad of this package, because it can wick solder away. Its land-pattern notes allow a via that is filled, plugged or tented. The PCB remark asks for copper-filled, planarised laser vias and for pads with vias in them to come out flat and solderable, which meets that note if it is applied to this via.

Where
Bottom: U7 (89.95, 141.5), pad 5 (AGND) with the laser via at (89.95, 141.5); U3 (110.25, 124.85), U4 (98.15, 124.85), U5 (89.95, 124.85) for comparison.
If left alone
If the via is left dimpled, the small paste deposit on U7's pad can be drawn into it, leaving U7, the VDD_CGEN regulator, weakly soldered or tilted. The risk is low with copper fill.
What to do
At JLC's file review, confirm that the copper-filled, planarised laser vias include the one in U7's pad. In a later layout, move the via off the pad as on U3, U4 and U5.

LDOA-67Note

Copper is spread unevenly between the layers (L2 70 %, L5 17 %), and the order remark forbids adding balancing copper inside the board.

Measured on the refilled board, copper covers L1 41 %, L2 70 %, L3 90 %, L4 90 %, L5 17 % and L6 53 % of the board area. L2 (the AGND plane) and L5 (lanes only, no pour) are mirror layers about the 0.83 mm core of the JLCH061611Y1-1078 stack-up. L5 sits on the 0.071 mm prepreg that the 106 L5-L6 laser vias pass through. The PCB remark says balancing copper elsewhere on the frame is fine but asks JLC to add none inside the board outline.

Where
L2 (L2_AGND_PLANE) and L5 (L5_SIG_RAILS) across the whole 22.64 x 36.76 mm board; order_frame_V4/JLC_Remarks_paste.txt (PCB remark).
If left alone
A thinly coppered L5 opposite a nearly full L2 raises the risk of bow and twist. It can also change the pressed thickness of the 0.071 mm L5-L6 dielectric, which sets the laser-via depth. On a 22.6 x 36.8 mm board the effect is probably small, and JLC may raise it at its file check.
What to do
At JLC's production-file confirmation, ask whether 17 % copper on L5 is acceptable for this stack-up. If JLC wants balancing copper, allow a non-functional hatch on L5 only, at least 0.5 mm from lanes, or accept JLC's own proposal.

Power & regulators 2

LDOA-22Note

The DC-bias loss of the ceramic capacitors on the +/-12 V and +/-8 V stimulation rails has not been checked against the regulator minimums.

The LT3094 needs at least 10 uF on its output and 10 uF on its input; the LT3042 needs 4.7 uF on each. U10 has C1002 (22 uF 1206) and C1004 (10 uF 0805) at -8 V, and one local 10 uF 0805 input capacitor, C1001, at -12 V (C21, 22 uF 0805, is on the same net through JP2). U9 has C902 (22 uF 1206) at +8 V and C901 (10 uF 0805) at +12 V (C14 through JP1). C14 and C21 are also U1's +/-12 V output capacitors, and the LT3582 data sheet warns that small 0805 and 0603 ceramics keep much less than their marked value at the working voltage. All are 25 V X5R parts whose product pages give no DC-bias curve.

Where
Bottom: C901 (95.45, 129.71), C902 (94.92, 122.95), C1001 (107.72, 129.71), C1002 (106.75, 122.95), C1004 (103.45, 122.85), C14 (92.7, 143.4), C21 (106.6, 142.8).
If left alone
If the effective capacitance falls below a minimum, a +/-8 V rail can ring, and low effective capacitance at C14/C21 raises the converter's +/-12 V ripple. Ringing is unlikely on the outputs; the single 10 uF input capacitors at 12 V are closest to the limit.
What to do
Get the maker's DC-bias data for CL21A106KAYNNNE, CL31A226KAHNNNE and CL21A226MAYNNNE (C14/C21) at 8 to 12 V, or measure. If C1001 keeps less than about 6 uF, fit 22 uF 1206 (C12891) at C1001 and C901.

Switching noise from the LT3582 on the stimulation rails and near REF_VCM must be measured.

Grounding & return paths 2

LDOA-02Note

The top-layer lanes use the L2 analog-ground plane as their return path, some clock lanes have no plane under them, and one data lane runs beside the REF_VDD pour.

L2 is the AGND plane, 0.071 mm under L1. About 277 mm of the 645 mm of top-layer signal lanes run over it, so their return current flows in AGND, which meets GND only at NT1. Where AGND is absent, the nearest plane is L3 GND, about 0.3 mm down. These lanes have no AGND under them: ASIC8_CLK32MHz_Out for all 9.2 mm, ASIC7_CLK32MHz_Out for 6.0 of 15.1 mm, ASIC5_CLK32MHz_Out for 7.2 of 19.7 mm, and ASIC7_8_TOP_SPI_CLK for 10.3 of 10.7 mm. On L1, ASIC7_DATA6 (0.1 mm wide) runs from J3 pin 58 at (103.18, 114.87) straight to (103.18, 127.5), and for 10.1 mm of that it lies 0.20 to 0.25 mm from the edge of the L1 REF_VDD pour. C55 (1 uF on REF_VDD, bottom) sits beside the middle of that run, about 1.2 mm away, so that point is well bypassed.

Where
L1 lanes over the L2_AGND_PLANE fill; ASIC7_DATA6 on L1 at x 103.18, y 114.87 to 127.5, beside the L1_PWR_ASIC_REF_VDD pour; C55 bottom (102.0, 122.7); NT1 bottom (100.58, 121.0); U2 (98.85, 135.6), U6 (104.5, 135.6), U8 (93.2, 135.6).

Signals, clocks & timing 1

LDOA-41Note

Noise margin on the CLK32 clock lanes and the stimulation control lines must be measured on a first board.

Each port's ASIC outputs return through J3/J4 pins 3, 5, 46, 48 (GND) and 42, 44 (AGND), and on the Frontend side through the 15 J1/J2 ground pins, 13 of which have their own via at or near the pad. On the bottom layer, ASIC1_CLK32MHz_Out runs 8.7 mm at a 0.10 mm gap between ASIC1_DATA5 and ASIC1_SYNC, and STIM_CLK_SHARED runs 8.0 mm between STIM_EN_SHARED and STIM_START_SHARED; STIM_EN_SHARED also runs 8.2 mm beside ASIC1_SYNC. Top-layer lanes lie directly over other nets' L2 lanes for about 147 mm in all, the longest ASIC7_DATA6 over STIM_CHIP3_SPI_DR (8.8 mm); ASIC7_CLK32MHz_Out lies 1.7 mm over ASIC3_4_TOP_SPI_LATCH and 0.7 mm over ASIC3_SPI_DL. The ASIC inputs are 1.5 V Schmitt triggers and its edges are about 1 ns or slower, but no threshold or drive figures are given.

Where
L6: ASIC1_CLK32MHz_Out from the J4.45 via near (109.69, 115.15) along y 113.36; STIM_CLK/EN/START_SHARED alongside. L1 over L2: ASIC7_DATA6 / STIM_CHIP3_SPI_DR, ASIC7_CLK32MHz_Out / ASIC3_4_TOP_SPI_LATCH and ASIC3_SPI_DL.

Mechanical, stack & thermal 1

LDOA-66Note

The 3D models of the two inductors and the four tiny regulators do not match the fitted parts, so the 3D stack files show them wrongly.

L11 and L12 use a 1.0 mm-tall Murata DFE201610P model, but the fitted TDK VLS201612HBX-6R8M-1 is up to 1.2 mm tall. U3, U4, U5 and U7 (1 x 1 mm X2SON packages, about 0.4 mm tall) have only a hand-made VRML model, which KiCad's STEP (3D CAD file) export skips, so they are missing from the STEP files and print models. Safety, ESD & bring-up 1

LDOA-47Note

The +12 V test pad TP2 is 0.6 mm from the enable pad TP4.

TP2 (STIM_POS_12V) and TP4 (EN_ASIC_RAILS) are 1.0 mm pads at 1.6 mm pitch, a 0.6 mm gap; TP5 (GND) is equally close on the other side. EN_ASIC_RAILS drives U1's SHDN pin (absolute maximum 6 V) and the enable pins of U3, U4, U5 and U7 (6.5 V). The Frontend does not cover these pads, so they are probed on a powered stack.

Where
Top: TP2 (92.5, 146.0), TP4 (94.1, 146.0), TP5 (92.5, 144.4), TP1 (94.1, 144.4).
If left alone
A probe slip bridging TP2 and TP4 puts about 12 V on these enable pins and can destroy U1 and the four TPS7A20 regulators, which sit on the bottom side.
What to do
Probe TP2 only with a fine spring tip, never a clip lead. In a later layout, move TP2 at least 2 mm from TP4.
