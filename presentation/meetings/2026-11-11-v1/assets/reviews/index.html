System and board review

The current boards, checked item by item on 19 November 2026. Open: 0 blockers, 0 majors, 38 minors, 72 notes.

Status on 19 November 2026. Every item was checked against HDMI Extension Board V39, Frontend 14 layers V13 (schematic V57), FPGA-LDO A layout V24 (schematic V43), ASIC boards V15 and pin map V8. PCBWay makes and assembles the ASIC boards; the other boards are made at JLC, where the Frontend and FPGA-LDO A go in break-off test frames. No blocker or major item is open. No fabricator has reviewed any of the boards, and nothing is ordered.
- Small changes still open in the files: a pin-1 mark at the Frontend's J1/J2 sockets (FE-62), the top-side stencil thickness in the Frontend and FPGA-LDO A order notes (FE-63, LDOA-62), and for the ASIC boards the missing handling lines in the order notes and an assembly panel (AB-20, AB-48).
- Before each board is first mated, check with an ohmmeter that the pins beside ±8 V and 3.3 V are not bridged (SYS-91).
- Written answers are still needed:
- JLC: accept the Frontend's stacked laser vias (asked by e-mail on 19 November, not answered yet), say how it turns bottom-side parts, and confirm the HDMI board's panel, finger thickness and smallest vias (FE-11, FE-49, LDOA-24, HDMI-60).
- PCBWay: say how it measures via spacing, and accept the 3 mil lines with ENEPIG and the fill of the small vias (AB-05, AB-20).
- ASIC designer: the bond diagram, the die height and the capacitor answers (AB-18, AB-06).
- Bonding house: accept the finger area and say how it will contain the encapsulant (AB-13, AB-14).
- Capacitor maker: DC-bias data for the stimulation-rail capacitors (LDOA-22).
- Heat, noise, cable current, the transceiver supply voltage and link margin can only be measured on the first built stack; those items are notes.

How this review was done

On 19 November 2026 every open item of the previous review was checked again against the current board files (the same files as the downloads on the board pages) and the records of what changed: nets, pads, values and positions in KiCad, the order files, and the makers' data sheets. Items that are fixed are removed. Items that can only be settled by building and measuring a board are notes. Nothing has been built or measured yet, no fabricator has reviewed the files, and nothing is ordered. A clean KiCad check means only that KiCad's rules pass. This review is not fabrication approval.
