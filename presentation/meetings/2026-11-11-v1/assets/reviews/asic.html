Pin map & connectors 2

AB-46Minor

The top schematic's DISC_OUT notes still say connector pins 3 and 5 keep their old names, and the die straps and capacitor bank are not explained beside the dies.

J3/J4 pins 3 and 5 are DGND on both boards: the netlist puts J3.3, J3.5, J4.3 and J4.5 on DGND, together with pins 46 and 48, and the J3/J4 note box on each schematic says so and cites the Hirose DF40 catalog. On the top schematic, the two DISC_OUT notes still say pins 3 and 5 'keep the names DISC_OUT(2) and DISC_OUT'; only a later sentence in the same box says they are DGND. The Datasheet field is empty on J1 and both dies. Nothing beside the die figures records their inherited strap ties, the same on both dies of both boards: EN_LDO to DGND; CGEN_RST and SPI_LDO_O, SPI_LDO_P, SPI_LDO_P1, SPI_LDO_P2 to DVDD; REF_1 to REF_4 to AGND. No source on record states their polarity. The 31 decoupling capacitors are drawn as one bank at the top of each sheet, with no power symbols, and an orphan no-connect flag sits inside that bank. They reach the dies, 170-500 mm further down the sheet, only through net labels. The project's schematic rules ask for: a two-item note (function; source and changes) beside each circuit, filled Datasheet fields, and support parts drawn beside the circuit they serve. The netlist itself is correct.

Where
Top schematic: DISC_OUT notes at (250, 366) and (250, 619). Both schematics: U01_BCD1 (145.415, 266.065) and U01_BCD2 (144.78, 515.62); capacitor bank x 210-384, y 25-102; orphan no-connect flag at (255.27, 53.34).
If left alone
A reader of the top schematic can take pins 3 and 5 for unused signal pins instead of ground. A later edit could change the strap ties without knowing that they set the on-chip regulator enable and the clock-generator reset.
What to do
One text-and-drawing pass on both schematics, with no netlist change. Delete the 'keep the names' sentence from the two DISC_OUT notes of the top schematic. Add a box beside each die figure saying the strap ties are inherited unchanged from the ASIC designer's original board and that their polarity is unconfirmed. Ask the designer about the polarity together with the bond diagram (AB-18). Fill the Datasheet fields of the dies and J1. Move each die's capacitor pairs beside that die, with power symbols at the bus ends. Delete the orphan no-connect flag.
Source
Schematics ASIC_Top_Board_V15.kicad_sch and ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15.kicad_sch (text boxes, J3/J4/U01 fields, netlist); pin map V8 (J3/J4 pins 3 and 5 = ground); Hirose DF40C-80DS-0.4V(51) drawing https://www.hirose.com/product/download?distributor=all&type=2d&lang=en&num=DF40C-80DS-0.4V%2851%29; Hirose DF40 catalogue https://www.hirose.com/en/product/series/DF40.

Layout & manufacturing 6

AB-05Minor

PCBWay has not yet said how it measures its 0.30 mm via spacing or accepted the 3 mil lines with ENEPIG; the via rings, hole-to-copper gaps and edge gaps meet its published limits.

Both boards are inside PCBWay's published via ring, hole-to-copper and edge limits, and the limits are DRC rules in each project, so KiCad checks them: through vias are 0.15 mm holes in 0.305 mm pads and laser (F.Cu-to-In1) vias are 0.10 mm holes in 0.255 mm pads, so every ring is at least 3 mil (0.0762 mm); every hole is at least 6 mil (0.1524 mm) from other-net copper, on the inner planes too, because unused inner via pads are removed; all copper is at least 0.20 mm from the routed edge (the outline is 22.37 x 17.03 mm). Two things only PCBWay can settle remain. Its help centre asks 0.30 mm between vias on different nets without saying how it is measured: centre to centre every pair is at least 0.359 mm apart, but edge to edge 117 (top) / 111 (bottom) pairs are closer, the closest at 0.233 mm. The 3 mil (0.0762 mm) lines are inside its HDI page value (0.065/0.065 mm) but outside its through-hole table. PCBWay offers ENEPIG and recommends it for gold wire bonding. The order notes put both questions to PCBWay; they are not answered yet.

Both boards. Closest different-net hole pairs about 0.233 mm edge to edge. 3 mil tracks on F.Cu, B.Cu and, on the top board, In7. Rules: ASIC_Top_Board_V15.kicad_dru and ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15.kicad_dru. Questions 2 and 3 of fab/PCBWay_Order_Notes_V15.txt.
If left alone
Without written acceptance PCBWay may hold the order for engineering questions or change the stack or copper on its own.
What to do
Send PCBWay the V15 files with the order notes and get written acceptance of: the via spacing (0.233 mm edge to edge at the closest); the 3 mil lines on the outer layers and In7; resin fill and cap on every via (AB-20); ENEPIG with the palladium and gold thickness the bonding house asks for.

AB-13Minor

The bond-finger area is tighter than the published wire-bond guides and has no written acceptance from the bonding house.

Each die has 176 fingers 0.1016 mm wide at 0.2032 mm pitch (128 side fingers 0.254 mm long, 45 top fingers 0.635 mm long). Würth's wire-bond rules ask at least 0.150 mm width, and the EPFL bonder guide asks for 0.300 x 0.500 mm pads. Between the two dies, 33 fingers are shared, each 0.378 mm long, and need one stitch from each die from opposite ends. The 8.1 mm die sits in an 8.583 mm gap between the side fingers, leaving about 0.24 mm per side. Besides the laser vias that sit in AGND fingers, 14 through-via rings reach into the finger-row mask openings (five by 0.013-0.063 mm, the rest by under 0.01 mm), and 68 (top) / 66 (bottom) more ring edges sit within 0.05 mm of an opening; every via is marked filled and capped.

Where
U01_BCD1 and U01_BCD2 on F.Cu, both boards. Shared fingers x 135.118-135.497, y 76.4-83.8. Deepest via rings inside openings: DGND (136.35, 81.85) 0.063 mm, VDD_ADC (135.81, 80.21) 0.055 mm, DVDD (125.28, 79.54) 0.034 mm, STIM_CHB (131.71, 74.87) 0.030 mm, VDD10 (135.69, 76.70) 0.013 mm.
If left alone
If the bonding house cannot bond the narrow or shared fingers, or rejects exposed via rings next to them, the dies cannot be bonded as drawn and a new footprint and board are needed.
What to do
Send the U01 finger geometry, the 33 shared fingers, the 0.24 mm die-to-finger gap and the exposed via rings to the bonding house and get a written yes. If refused, widen or stagger the fingers and move the intruding vias.

AB-20Minor

The ASIC order notes leave out the probe-edge, reflow-order, cleaning and die-area instructions, and PCBWay has not said how it fills the small vias.

The PCBWay order notes ask for every via to be filled and capped, name ENEPIG and wire bonding, ask for the bond pads to be kept free of flux and ink, and give the J3/J4 part numbers. Every via on both boards is marked filled and capped, including the vias under the dies, the in-pad vias at C50 and C6, and the vias beside the 0201 capacitor pads (one AGND via at (139.755, 81.785) overlaps the C46 pad; filled and capped, it is a normal via in pad). KiCad's fill marks do not appear in the standard drill or copper Gerbers, so PCBWay fills vias only because the order asks: its 'Via in pad' option plugs them with resin and plates a cap over them. Its pages disagree on the smallest hole it resin-fills (0.254 mm on the HDI page, 0.15 mm on the advanced page), so the fill for 0.15 mm and 0.10 mm holes in this 0.8 mm board needs its answer; the notes ask only whether the via structure can be built. The notes do not tell the fabricator or assembler to: keep break-off tabs and mouse bites off the probe edge; solder the bottom side first and the top second, with no third reflow and no wet cleaning of the DF40s; keep solder mask over the whole area under each die. The die attach, done after assembly, must be non-conductive, and no document says so.

Where
fab/PCBWay_Order_Notes_V15.txt; both boards: all 527-528 vias (marked filled and capped); C46 pad 2 via (139.755, 81.785); probe edge y 84.472; J3 (B.Cu) and J4 (F.Cu).
If left alone
A tab stub on the probe edge can hold the probe off the die, and cleaning or flux residue weakens the bonds and the connector contacts. If PCBWay cannot fill the 0.10 mm and 0.15 mm holes, solder can wick into the in-pad vias and the die attach sits over open holes.
What to do
Add to the order notes: no tabs or mouse bites on the probe edge, the reflow order with no third reflow, no wet cleaning of the connectors, and solder mask kept under the dies. Write the non-conductive die attach into the bonding instructions. Ask PCBWay which fill it uses for 0.15 mm and 0.10 mm holes in a 0.8 mm board.

AB-48Minor

There is no assembly panel, fiducial mark or tooling hole for the small two-sided ASIC boards, and PCBWay must panelize boards this small.

Each board is 22.37 x 17.03 mm and 0.8 mm thick, with parts on both faces: J4 and 7 capacitors on F.Cu, J3 and 24 capacitors on B.Cu. The capacitors are 0201s and the DF40s have 0.4 mm pitch. Neither board has a fiducial (an optical alignment mark for the placement machine), a non-plated tooling hole or a panel. The Frontend and FPGA-LDO A boards, made at JLC, each sit in their own test frame; nothing like it exists for the ASIC boards. PCBWay, which makes and assembles the ASIC boards, has these panel rules:
- Boards smaller than 50 x 100 mm must be panelized for assembly.
- Break-away rails go on the two long edges whenever copper is closer than 3.5 mm to the edge. Here every edge qualifies.
- Rails are at least 5 mm wide and usually carry three 1.0 mm fiducials with 1.7 mm mask openings and four tooling holes.
- When both faces carry parts, each face needs its own fiducials.
- Tab-routed boards need 1.6 mm gaps and stamp holes of at least 0.45 mm.
- V-scoring is only for boards larger than 45 x 60 mm.
- Fiducials on the single boards are asked for but may be left off when there is no room. Each mark must sit at least 5 mm from the board edge, which leaves almost no room on these boards.
Every edge has something near it:
- East edge: the C50 and C6 pads are 0.41-0.43 mm in, and CLKH runs on B.Cu 0.46 mm in between y 71.6 and 74.9.
- Probe edge: C59 and C55 are 0.57-0.67 mm in, and this edge must stay clean (AB-20).
- West, east and probe edges: the finger-row openings end 0.41-0.70 mm in (AB-14).
- Connector edge: C18, C19, C25 and C26 are 0.81 mm in, the not-fitted J1 pad 0.42 mm and the inner planes 0.25 mm.

Where
Both boards: outline x 124.139-146.507, y 67.44-84.472. Edge-near items: C50 pad 2 and C6 pad 2 (east edge, y 70-72.3); CLKH on B.Cu at x 146.01; C59 and C55 (probe edge); C18, C19, C25, C26 (connector edge, y 68.17); J1 (144.875, 68.28).
If left alone
Without a supplied panel, PCBWay builds its own and may put tabs and mouse bites where they cut edge copper, crack C50/C6 or leave stubs on the probe edge. With no fiducials on the board, placing the 0201s and DF40s on both sides depends on whatever marks PCBWay adds to its rails.
What to do
Build an ASIC panel to PCBWay's rules. Both board types can share it. It needs rails of at least 5 mm on the long sides, three 1.0 mm fiducials with 1.7 mm mask openings on each face, four tooling holes, and tab routes with 1.6 mm gaps (no V-score). Put tabs only on the connector edge (y 67.44), away from J1, and specify cut, not snapped. Keep tabs off the probe edge, the east edge (C50/C6 and CLKH) and the finger rows. If PCBWay builds the panel instead ('Panel by Supplier'), give it these limits in writing. Add the panel or the limits to the ASIC order package and the AB-20 notes.
Source
Board files ASIC_Top_Board_V15.kicad_pcb and ASIC_Bottom_Board_V15.kicad_pcb (footprints, Edge.Cuts, no fiducial or NPTH); PCBWay assembly special reminders (panelization under 50 x 100 mm, rails when copper is within 3.5 mm of the edge) https://www.pcbway.com/assembly-special-remind.html; PCBWay panel requirements for assembly (fiducials, tooling holes, tab routes) https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/Panel_Requirements_for_Assembly.html; PCBWay panel creation (5 mm rails, V-score limits) https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/PCB_Panel_Creation.html; PCBWay design guide, breakaway rails, fiducial marks and tooling holes (fiducials on each populated side, 5 mm from the edge) https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/design_instruction/PCB_Panelization__Breakaway_Rails__Fiducial_Marks__Tooling_Holes.html; PCBWay help centre, requests for machine assembly https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/pcb_assembly_ordering/What_are_the_requests_on_pcb_for_assembly_by_machine_.html.

The ASIC designer owes the bond diagram and has not yet checked the capacitor values.

AB-06Minor

The die, probe and their keep-outs are not drawn on the footprint, and the upper board's capacitors leave only about 1.0-1.17 mm above each die of the board below.

The U01 footprints hold only fingers and mask openings: no die outline, centre, orientation mark, probe outline, courtyard or copper keep-out. DRC with all rules turned on reports the missing U01 courtyards. In a stack, the upper board's 24 B.Cu 0201 capacitors sit directly over the dies of the lower board (x 129.55-141.0, y 79.9-83.35). Solder mask, die attach, the die, the bond-wire loops rising above the die and the probe base must all fit in that space. Below the dies, the lowest fingers end 0.457 mm from the probe edge, and that strip carries F.Cu copper and vias with no keep-out. Die thickness, bond-loop height and probe-base height are not recorded.

Where
Both boards: U01_BCD1 (130.369, 80.205) and U01_BCD2 (140.755, 80.205); B.Cu capacitors C1, C2, C4, C5, C7, C14, C33, C34, C41, C42, C46, C48, C51-C62; strip y 84.015-84.472.

Signals, clocks & timing 2

AB-40Note

One FPGA pin drives the CLKH clock inputs of four dies, and up to 16 die pads on the shared control lines, while the die's input load is unknown.

CHIP_RESET, FE_RESET and STIM_CHB reach 4 die pads per ASIC board. The FPGA-LDO A board joins both ports, so one FPGA-side pin drives 16 pads. STIM_EN, STIM_CLK, STIM_START, IMP_TEST and AC_IN reach 8 each. CLKH, the dies' master clock, has no buffer. Each port's line comes from one FPGA ball (V17 for ASIC5-8 on J3, G16 for ASIC1-4 on J4) and crosses FPGA-LDO A with only a pull-down (R21, R20). It reaches four die CLKH pads, because J3.7 and J4.7 pass straight through the near board to the far one. Each die's returned clock is derived from CLKH. The ASIC designer does not know the pads' input capacitance or leakage, and advised against sharing one line among many chips. The follow-up question, whether two CLKH lines per head are acceptable, has no answer on record.

Where
ASIC nets CHIP_RESET (J3.71), FE_RESET (J3.65), STIM_CHB (J3.49), STIM_CLK (J3.53), CLKH (J3.7 = J4.7, U01_BCD1 and U01_BCD2 CLKH); FPGA-LDO A CHIP_RESET_SHARED and STIM_CLK_SHARED to J3 and J4, BOARD3_CLK (J1.2 to J3.7, R21) and BOARD1_CLK (J1.42 to J4.7, R20).
If left alone
A slow or ringing CHIP_RESET or STIM_CLK edge reaches all eight chips of a head at once. A poor CLKH edge at the far die upsets the clock of four ASICs.
What to do
Get the ASIC designer's answer on two CLKH lines per head. If the answer is no, give each board its own CLKH contact (a pin map change) or add a buffer on FPGA-LDO A. In any case, during bring-up with all boards fitted, scope CLKH at the far die pad and CHIP_RESET and STIM_CLK at the far ASIC board.

Measure on a built board
AB-49Note

On each ASIC board the master clock CLKH runs its last 9.8 mm on the top layer just outside the edge of every plane, beside the probe edge.

CLKH is the dies' master clock, and one line reaches four dies (AB-40). On each board it runs from J4 to the second die on B.Cu. From there, one F.Cu segment 0.0762 mm wide and 9.84 mm long at y 84.23 joins the second die's CLKH finger (135.368, 83.955) to the first die's finger (124.982, 83.955). Every inner plane under that strip (In1 AGND, In2 VDD_FE, In3 REF_VDD, In4 REF_VCM, In5 VDD_DRIVER, In6 VDD_ADC, In7 DVDD, In8 DGND) ends at y 84.222. The trace centre is therefore 0.008 mm outside every plane, and about 60% of its width has no plane under it. Fill sampling finds 10.3 mm of its F.Cu run not over the In1 plane. Its outer edge is 0.204 mm from the probe edge. Several other top-layer control lines also return through the In1 analog-ground plane, while AGND and DGND are separate nets on this board: FE_RESET runs 17.3 mm over In1 AGND (top board), CHIP_RESET 6.3-7.8 mm, and STIM_CHB, STIM_CLK and STIM_EN 5-8 mm.

Where
Both boards: CLKH F.Cu segment (125.257, 84.23)-(135.093, 84.23); plane outlines end at y 84.222; probe edge y 84.472.
If left alone
The clock's return current crowds at the plane edge next to the board edge. This raises loop inductance and impedance and increases radiation and coupling into nearby lines. The FPGA-driven control edges return through the front-end's analog ground before they reach digital ground at the star tie on FPGA-LDO A. Each effect is probably small, but none has been measured.
What to do
Route the die-to-die CLKH leg on B.Cu or In7 next to the In8 DGND plane, as the J4-to-die leg already runs on B.Cu, and via up at each finger. If it stays on F.Cu, move it at least 0.2 mm inside the plane outlines. At bring-up, scope CLKH at the first die's finger, and AGND-to-DGND noise during readout.
